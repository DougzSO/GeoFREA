\section*{Notation}
\label{sec:notation}

The symbols below are used from Section~\ref{sec:h1} (H1) to the scenario
discovery of the synthesis chapter. A symbol keeps its meaning in every section;
where a section adds a subscript or a superscript, the base symbol is the one
listed. ``Defined'' names the subsection where the definition and its equation
are given. The last column is the name of the same quantity in
\texttt{docs/METHODOLOGY.md} and in the tables of GeoFREA.

Three symbols are reused with another meaning in a different part of the text and
are therefore marked in the table: $m$ is the rank tolerance in H1b, H3, H4 and
PRIM and the climate member in the definition of a future; $\alpha$ is the core
threshold of H1a and the peeling fraction of PRIM; $\theta$ is the case-of-interest
threshold of PRIM and $\theta_h$ the exposure threshold of a hazard channel in H5b.
