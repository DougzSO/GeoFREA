## Active implementation decisions

- **D-X-001 — The phase reads persisted artifacts only, computes its own scale suffix and reads the IRENA file through the configuration fingerprint (2026-11-23, COMMAND 210 and 210C; DEC-098).** The nominal LCOE and the P90 per cell and member (F6), the energy and the capacity factor (F5), the classes, the target conversion `g_twh` of H5 and the `V_f` of each future (F7) are all persisted, so no kernel is needed and no module of F6 or F7 is imported. The directory suffix of a scale other than 0.05 degree is written in `exploratory_dir` and not in `core.scale.SCALE_DEPENDENT_PHASES`: an edit of that module changes the code fingerprint of every phase; a test requires the suffix to equal the one `core.scale` gives for every configured scale. `irena_reference.yaml` entered `CONFIG_FILES` and the scope of the phase (`editions.irena_rpgc_2024.life_years` and the pair of the country), so a change of the reference plant makes the phase run again and no other. `prim_vf` and `tau_cell_basis` are optional products: a pair with no reference plant, or a null `prim.outcome_threshold`, skips them and says why.
- **D-X-002 — Mechanical labels beside the label of DEC-098 (2026-11-23, COMMAND 210C).** The DEC fixes one label, "material climate pressure on the cost of the target", for `Delta_MC >= 0.05` at the target. The code also writes "no material climate pressure on the cost of the target" (`Delta_MC` below 0.05) and "target level not reached by the reference member" (no finite `Delta_MC`) so that every row of the target level has a label. They are mechanical complements of the declared label and do not change the rule: the threshold 0.05 and the level at which it applies are those of the DEC; the labels exist only at the target level of the nominal basis.
- **D-X-003 — The O&M of tau on the cell basis (2026-11-23, COMMAND 210 and 210C; DEC-098 d).** The reference plant keeps the TIC and the capacity factor of IRENA and the central discount rate; the lifetime is that of the nominal sample of F6 and the O&M is `opex_fixed_frac x capex(s0)` of that sample (USD/kW/yr) in place of the IRENA O&M. The capex of F6 is the TIC of IRENA in the six pairs (PRT 779 and 1,659, IND 525 and 1,110, BRA 724 and 1,307, in `parameters.json` and in the design matrices of the real runs; D1), so `opex_fixed_frac x capex(s0)` is `opex_fixed_frac x TIC` and the reading of the DEC has one answer; two tests check it. `tau_life_only` and `tau_om_only` change one of the two terms, for the reading.
- **D-X-004 — The run on the three countries and its internal checks (2026-11-23, COMMAND 211).** `exploratory_analysis` and `results_synthesis` ran on PRT, IND and BRA with `--real-run` (BRA with `GEOFREA_MEMORY_CAPS=0`); BRA peaked at 2,081 MB (IND 1,070, PRT 346) and `results_synthesis` at 2,149 MB. After a snapshot of the hashes of every output: the `content_sha256` of the 1,427 artifacts of F1 to F7b in the manifests is unchanged (only `results_synthesis` differs); the four cross-country artifacts are unchanged; the tables of T-R1 to T-R12, T-O and T-M are byte-identical and the figures equal pixel by pixel to those of `results_synthesis` run on the commit before the phase (7ba48c4) on a copy of the outputs; only T-X1 to T-X4 and the 21 artifacts per country of the phase are new, and the provenance of T-M1 gains the three edges of the phase. Internal checks: (i) `MC_m0` at `G_target` reproduces the LCOE of the marginal cell of the conversion of H5 in IND solar (28.2226, difference 0); in BRA solar the target (90 GW) exceeds the tau-domain of H5, so the conversion takes the capacity factor of the whole domain and has no marginal cell (`MC_m0` 44.8320, the cell of 90 GW by cheapest-first 44.8291, difference 0.003, 0.007%); in IND wind the target is not reached by the reference and `MC_m0` is empty; PRT and BRA wind have no target in GW. (ii) `VUL_clim` is not non-negative: the reference member `m0` is not one of the core members, so a cell whose LCOE falls in every core member has a negative value (PRT solar 1,401 cells, minimum -0.026; BRA solar 14, -0.0008; BRA wind 46,934, -0.086, checked on cell 13445013: LCOE 905 at f0, 545 to 827 in the 15 members); IND solar has 331 climate-fragile cells with no value; `VUL >= VUL_clim` and `VUL_tec > 0` everywhere. The expectation of the command assumed `m0` among the members; L-264. (iii) The Spearman correlations of VUL_clim, VUL_tec and VUL with LCOE at f0 and with MR are in `vulnerability_summary_*` (group `spearman`).
- **D-X-005 — The target of H5c without tau (2026-11-24, COMMAND 213A; DEC-100).** COMMAND 213 showed that the conversion of H5 that DEC-098 named for `G_target` depends on tau: when the target exceeds the capacity of Omega_econ (IND wind 13.6 GW against 138 GW, BRA solar 12.3 GW against 90 GW), the capacity factor of the few best cells is extended to the whole target, and the IND wind target came out above the energy of the whole F7 set. `h5c.target_energy` takes the cells of the F7 set with a finite LCOE at `f0`, a positive energy and a positive capacity, by `LCOE_i,f0` ascending (ties by `cell_id`), until their accumulated capacity reaches the target in GW, the last cell fractional; a target above their capacity gives the whole set and `target_above_set_capacity`. The pipeline reads `target_value` and `target_unit` of the primary variant of `h5_summary` (a target in another unit has no level, as before) and no longer reads tau for this. Table schema 1.1: `g_target_h5_twh` (the old conversion, target level only, for comparison) and `target_above_set_capacity`; T-X1 shows both. Tests: independence of tau (tau 5 and 1000 give the same `G_target`), the flag, a hand-made order with a tie and a fractional cell, and the synthetic run (MC at the target is the marginal cell of the tau-free order). Run on PRT, IND and BRA (2026-11-24, COMMAND 213A; BRA peak 1,780 MB, under the cap of 2,624 MB): the content hashes of the 1,424 artifacts of every other phase (F1 to F7b, the maps, cross_country) are those of the snapshot, T-R1 to T-R12 are identical (the tables, figures and the other thesis files are unchanged; only the `methodology_version` stamp of the provenance files and of the text of T-R8 moves, 19.4.18 to 19.4.19), and only the `h5c_*` tables and T-X1 change. IND wind: `G_target` 257.2 TWh/year (77.6% of the energy of the F7 set at `m0`) against 375.5 TWh/year of the conversion of H5 (113%), `Delta_MC` 19.0% and the label "material climate pressure" (the reference member did not reach the old target); BRA solar: 160.3 against 162.6 TWh/year, `Delta_MC` 2.2% (unchanged label); IND solar is unchanged (759.4 TWh/year, 7.8%).
