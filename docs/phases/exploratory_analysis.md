## Active implementation decisions

- **D-X-001 — The phase reads persisted artifacts only, computes its own scale suffix and reads the IRENA file through the configuration fingerprint (2026-11-23, COMMAND 210 and 210C; DEC-098).** The nominal LCOE and the P90 per cell and member (F6), the energy and the capacity factor (F5), the classes, the target conversion `g_twh` of H5 and the `V_f` of each future (F7) are all persisted, so no kernel is needed and no module of F6 or F7 is imported. The directory suffix of a scale other than 0.05 degree is written in `exploratory_dir` and not in `core.scale.SCALE_DEPENDENT_PHASES`: an edit of that module changes the code fingerprint of every phase; a test requires the suffix to equal the one `core.scale` gives for every configured scale. `irena_reference.yaml` entered `CONFIG_FILES` and the scope of the phase (`editions.irena_rpgc_2024.life_years` and the pair of the country), so a change of the reference plant makes the phase run again and no other. `prim_vf` and `tau_cell_basis` are optional products: a pair with no reference plant, or a null `prim.outcome_threshold`, skips them and says why.
- **D-X-002 — Mechanical labels beside the label of DEC-098 (2026-11-23, COMMAND 210C).** The DEC fixes one label, "material climate pressure on the cost of the target", for `Delta_MC >= 0.05` at the target. The code also writes "no material climate pressure on the cost of the target" (`Delta_MC` below 0.05) and "target level not reached by the reference member" (no finite `Delta_MC`) so that every row of the target level has a label. They are mechanical complements of the declared label and do not change the rule: the threshold 0.05 and the level at which it applies are those of the DEC; the labels exist only at the target level of the nominal basis.
- **D-X-003 — The O&M of tau on the cell basis (2026-11-23, COMMAND 210 and 210C; DEC-098 d).** The reference plant keeps the TIC and the capacity factor of IRENA and the central discount rate; the lifetime is that of the nominal sample of F6 and the O&M is `opex_fixed_frac x capex(s0)` of that sample (USD/kW/yr) in place of the IRENA O&M. The capex of F6 is the TIC of IRENA in the six pairs (PRT 779 and 1,659, IND 525 and 1,110, BRA 724 and 1,307, in `parameters.json` and in the design matrices of the real runs; D1), so `opex_fixed_frac x capex(s0)` is `opex_fixed_frac x TIC` and the reading of the DEC has one answer; two tests check it. `tau_life_only` and `tau_om_only` change one of the two terms, for the reading.
