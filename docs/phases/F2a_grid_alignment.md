## Active implementation decisions

- **D-F2a-029 — The distance layers measure the exact WGS84 geodesic (2026-11-09, COMMANDS 115 to 117; METHODOLOGY 19.0.0, M-F2a-03 and M-F2a-09; OQ-076).** The isotropic scale (`calculate_wgs84_isotropic_distance`) and the rasterization of the features (`rasterize_in_chunks`) are removed; the five layers `grid`, `poi`, `poi_sensitivity`, `roads`, `rivers` use `feature_vertices` and `geodesic_distance_to_vertices` (method M3 of `docs/_audit/dist_scale_prototype.md`). The rivers of BRA (26.5 million vertices) go in blocks of 2 million with one tree alive at a time, an upper bound from a real vertex per strip, and the vector freed once its vertices are taken: 1,221 MB and 121 s in the layer process (cap 1,536 MB; the first version, one block of 6 million with the vector held, peaked at 1,583 MB and did not finish in 1,400 s). The code of F2a changed, so the five aligned distance layers were recomputed in PRT, IND and BRA; `poi_counts.json` did not change; validation on real data in the report of COMMAND 116.
- **D-F2a-028 — Slope and slope bins from FABDEM; the elevation stays on GLO-30 (2026-11-06, COMMAND 101; METHODOLOGY 18.0.0, M-F2a-07 and M-F2a-08; test `docs/_audit/slope_dem_test_100.md`, canopy diagnostic `docs/_audit/slope_canopy_99.md`).** (a) Source: FABDEM V1-2 tiles (`scripts/acquire_fabdem.py`, `fetchers/fabdem.py`): the 10 degree zips are read by HTTP Range (directory and member only; 26.25 GB for 1,186 tiles, 1,193 with the 7 of the test; stop above 40 GB), a block the server drops is asked for again, each tile is written whole and the manifest `external/fabdem/fabdem_manifest.json` (zip URL, size and ETag, tile size and SHA-256, date, licence text and its SHA-256) is rewritten after every tile. (b) Layer: new `aligned_layer.fabdem_slope` (`layers.build_fabdem_slope`, `fabdem_slope.fabdem_slope_outputs`) writes `<ISO3>_slope_fabdem_aligned.tif` and `<ISO3>_slope_counts_fabdem_aligned.tif` in one pass, the same arithmetic as the GLO-30 pass (`_glo30_strip_samples`, 41 bins, pixel by sample centre), with a checkpoint every 32 rows; the result fields `slope` and `slope_counts` point to them. With no FABDEM tile acquired the slope is GLO-30's, as before, with a warning. (c) Elevation: its layer, its cache key and its code fingerprint are those of before (`da77f0af...`; test `test_the_code_fingerprint_in_the_key_of_the_elevation_layer_is_the_one_it_had_before_command_101`). The new layer is registered by a statement after the dict `CACHE_FUNCTION_ENTRIES` and not inside it: `cache_code` reaches the dict, so one more key in the literal changes the key of the layers that clip a vector (roads, lakes, rivers); the test `test_registering_the_slope_layer_changed_the_key_of_no_other_layer` holds it, and the import of `fabdem_slope` is inside the builder for the same reason. (d) Fallback: a 1 degree square with no FABDEM tile (open sea, or not fetched) is read from GLO-30; the squares and the country area they cover (`fallback_area_km2`, `fallback_share`) are in `<ISO3>_slope_dem_source.json`, in the F2a output (`slope_source`) and in the log. All 1,193 squares of PRT, IND and BRA have a FABDEM tile. Round of 2026-11-06: layer `slope_fabdem` 42 s (PRT, 462 MB), 1,911 s (IND, 827 MB), 4,177 s (BRA, 942 MB); `elevation` cached in the three countries; `slope_source` records 19, 357 and 817 FABDEM squares and no fallback. (e) Seam: a GLO-30 square beside a FABDEM square gives the stencil at the edge two different surfaces. Result of the test on seven tiles: E4 at the PV thresholds falls to 0.17 to 0.91 of the GLO-30 value and the PV eligible area rises 18 % (3 %), 9 % (5 %) and 2 % (10 %); wind changes by at most 2 %. - **D-F2a-004 — Distances are stored uncapped; `distance_cap_km` only flags (OQ-040, METHODOLOGY 2.0.0, Douglas's authorization 2026-10-06).** D-F2a-002 unified the cap at 100 km, which was inert while distances only fed 5-30 km proximity decays. Once distances became monetized cost drivers (M-F6-01) a cap understates the connection CAPEX, and the LCOE, of remote cells. The literature gives no universal distance limit and puts distance into site cost continuously (`docs/_audit/2026-10_distance_connection_cost_evidence.md`). So the rasters keep the real geodesic distance, the old constant `LINEAR_FEATURE_MAX_DIST_KM` is removed, and `distance_cap_km` (a `settings.yaml` value, 100 km provisional and unsourced, L-019) only defines the flag raster written beside each distance raster, a quality indicator (the eligible-area share beyond it is reported per cell by F3). Douglas's stated direction for the cost side, beyond this change, is to model connection cost as a function of the real distance and of infrastructure characteristics (OQ-041); that needs sourced data per country and is not done here.

- **D-F2a-005 — Wind is aligned per height; the AHP combination is removed (G-2, 2026-10-06).** M-F2a-04 forbids combining heights in F2a. The legacy `combine_wind_layers()` blended 50/100/200 m wind speed with AHP weights (Saaty, 1980, unsourced judgments) into one raster, and only for wind speed. Now every GWA product acquired in F1 (M-F1-03: wind speed, Weibull A, Weibull k, air density at 100, 150 and 200 m, 12 layers) is reprojected on its own, which is what F2b/H-4 and F5 (Weibull A/k and air density per hub height, M-F5-03) need. `GridAlignmentResult.wind` (one raster) is replaced by `wind_layers` (a dict keyed `<product>_<height>m`); older manifests carrying `wind` no longer validate and need `rerun_phases` for `grid_alignment` (PRT's entry was already stale). The legacy F2b criterion `wind_resource` now receives the 100 m wind speed (one height, not a combination) until H-4 replaces it with the per-height resource layers.

- **D-F2a-006 — Slope uses geodesic pixel spacing (G-1, 2026-10-06).** `derive_slope_from_dem()` kept its method (central-difference `numpy.gradient`, nodata masked before the gradient) and replaced the spherical spacing (`dy = res_y * 111.32 km/deg`, `dx = res_x * 111.32 * cos(lat)`) with the WGS84 metres per degree at each row's latitude (`core.geodesy`), as M-F2a-02 requires. Measured effect on the native DEMs (2026-10-06, whole DEM, new minus old): PRT 622,393 valid pixels, max |difference| 0.055 degree, p99 0.021, median 0.0016, mean slope 2.5275 -> 2.5286; BRA 52,118,725 valid pixels, max 0.190 degree, p99 0.061, median 0.0010, mean slope 1.9050 -> 1.9097. The change is small (largest where slopes are steep); the V-01 fixtures still need the G-4 refreeze, which needs Douglas's authorization. The Horn 8-neighbour method (as in `gdaldem`) was not adopted; that would be a separate decision.
- **D-F2a-007 — The adaptive-resolution mode and the duplicated schema defaults are removed (G-3, 2026-10-06).** The method fixes the resolution (M-F2a-01, 0.01 degree snapped for 5 x 5 nesting); the legacy "adaptive" computation was never the configured value and its `target_pixels` had no calibration. `AdaptiveResolutionConfig`, the `adaptive` settings block and `GridAlignmentInputs.adaptive_*` are deleted. Resolution and the distance flag threshold are required, no-default values read only from `settings.yaml` (CONVENTIONS).

- **D-F2a-008 — F2a follows the A-08 layout (G-3, 2026-10-06).** Aligned rasters, the distance flag rasters and `<ISO3>_grid_metadata.json` are written to `outputs/<ISO3>/grid_alignment/artifacts/` (they used to sit directly in `outputs/<ISO3>/grid_alignment/`); the vector clip cache and the native-resolution slope temp file go to `interim/<ISO3>/grid_alignment/` through `core.paths.interim()` (they used to share `outputs/<ISO3>/processed/` with data_quality_audit by disk-file convention). Consequence: F2a no longer reuses F1b's clip cache and recomputes the clips once per country (a time cost only); moving F1b's own `processed/` cache is F1b's decision. Tests isolate `GEOFREA_DATA_DIR` per test so the interim cache cannot leak between tests.

- **D-F2a-009 — GWA Weibull A/k tiles carry no CRS; the CRS comes from the same-height wind-speed tile, or the phase fails (2026-10-06).** A PRT run of `grid_alignment` stopped at `weibull_a_100m` with a rasterio CRS error. Scan of `raw/gwa/`: all Weibull A and k files (3 countries x 3 heights x 2 products) declare no CRS, while wind-speed and air-density files declare EPSG:4326, on the same grid (the GWA API serves the Weibull tiles untagged). `reproject_to_grid()` now raises `MissingSourceCrsError` for any source without a CRS unless a `src_crs` is given, and `alignment.py::_gwa_crs_for()` supplies the wind-speed sibling's CRS only if its transform and shape equal the layer's own, else it raises. The raw files are not rewritten. Tests in `test_grid_alignment_raster_alignment.py` (2 new). This was a real F1 data gap exposed by G-2: until then only the 100 m wind speed was aligned.

- **D-F2a-013 — Weibull k below 1 checked and not a defect (2026-10-07; closes the H-6 check noted in D-F2a-011).** On the aligned 100 m layers (every 4th pixel): the three GWA products agree with each other, since `U = A * Gamma(1 + 1/k)` holds to better than 0.1% for the median pixel in IND and BRA, so no CRS, scale or nodata fault. Pixels with k < 1: IND 1,053 of 163,177 (0.65%, minimum 0.61, mostly lat 24-33 N, the Himalaya and the north-east, median wind speed 2.3 m/s), BRA 88 of 444,373 (0.02%, minimum 0.85), PRT none (minimum 1.01). They are GWA's own values in sheltered low-wind terrain; the Weibull mean-power formula is valid for any k > 0 and these cells sit far below any capacity-factor cut. Median k is 2.0 (PRT), 2.05 (IND), 2.2 (BRA). No code change.
- **D-F2a-014 — Population is summed, not sampled (2026-10-07, M-F2a-05, Douglas's authorization).** The aligned population held 1/144 of the real count because `reproject_to_grid` used bilinear resampling at a 12 x downsampling (about one 100 m sample per pixel). `alignment.py` now passes `Resampling.sum`. Measured after rerunning F2a: the sum of `<ISO3>_population_aligned.tif` is 1.012e7 for PRT (0.982 of about 1.03e7), 1.387e9 for IND (0.991 of 1.40e9) and 2.163e8 for BRA (1.015 of 2.13e8). Test `test_sum_resampling_keeps_the_total_of_a_count_raster_when_coarsening` (12 x 12 unit pixels give 144 per pixel, the old default gives 1). The V-01 fixture was refrozen with the same authorization: only the population layer of ZZZ changes (30 of 600 pixels). The aligned-raster cache is keyed by file existence only, so the old population files had to be deleted before the rerun (a changed method does not invalidate it); the rerun took 19 s (PRT), 14.5 min (IND) and 39 min (BRA). Related notes (land cover sampled at a point, slope as a pixel mean) are in D-F3-004.
- **D-F2a-015 — Land cover as per-class sample counts (2026-10-07, M-F2a-06, approved by Douglas).** New aligned layer `<ISO3>_land_cover_counts_aligned.tif` (`raster_alignment.land_cover_class_counts`): 11 uint16 bands, one per WorldCover class, each pixel holding the number of 10 m samples of that class (14,400 per 0.01 degree pixel; nodata 65535 outside the country; classes and samples per pixel in the file tags). Counting is a block count over the native tiles (a 0.01 degree pixel is 120 x 120 samples, tiles sit on the 0.05 degree lattice), so it is exact; a tile that is not on the grid edges, or a tile that cannot be read inside the country, raises. The point-sample `land_cover` stays for display. Check on PRT: the country share of every class equals the point-sample share to within 0.2 points (forest 29.7% vs 29.8%, grassland 49.4% vs 49.4%), so the point sample was unbiased, but its class equals the dominant class of the pixel in only 70% of the pixels, which is the cell-level noise this removes. Tests: exact counts on a synthetic tile (`test_grid_alignment_raster_alignment.py`), fractional E5 in `test_land_eligibility_pipeline.py`. **IND addendum (2026-10-07):** the 91 IND land-cover tiles hold 3340 x 3340 samples per 3 degrees (0.000898 degree, about 100 m), not the 10 m of the global product, and 0.01 degree is not a whole multiple of that; the exact block count refused them. `land_cover_class_counts` now has a second mode, chosen per country from the tile headers: each sample is counted into the pixel holding its centre (about 123 samples per pixel, approximate at pixel edges) with an extra last band for samples of no class (tag `samples_per_pixel` 0), and E5 then divides by the sum of all bands. Where the IND tiles came from, and whether they were resampled from 10 m, is not recorded (open).
- **D-F2a-016 — Slope as 30 m bin counts from Copernicus GLO-30 (2026-10-07, M-F2a-07, approved by Douglas, who had asked for the share above the threshold).** The premise of D-F3-004 was wrong: the DEM held by F1 (`database/raw/elevation/<country>/<ISO3>_elevation.tif`) is a 0.005 degree resample (about 550 m; PRT file 0.005, IND 0.005, BRA 0.005), so the slope F2a derived from it is a 550 m slope and the earlier E4 (3.4% of PRT for solar at 10 degrees) was an underestimate, not a "pixel mean of 30 m slopes". New acquisition `fetchers/copernicus_dem30.py` + `scripts/acquire_dem30.py` (public AWS bucket `copernicus-dem-30m`, no account, about 42 MB per 1 degree tile; 404 means open sea; Content-Length checked, sha256 in the manifest). New aligned layer `<ISO3>_slope_counts_aligned.tif` (`raster_alignment.slope_class_counts`): 41 uint16 bands, band i = number of 30 m samples with slope in [i, i+1) degrees, last band 40 degrees and above; samples counted into the grid pixel holding their centre (edge ties go below and east); a sample whose stencil touches a missing value counts in no band; nodata 65535 outside the country.
