## Active implementation decisions

- **D-F3-001 — `cell_id` is a global lattice index, with `row`, `col` and the cell center stored beside it (2026-10-06, Douglas's verdict).** The decision unit is a 0.05 degree cell (S-06, 5 x 5 pixels of the 0.01 degree grid, M-F2a-01). Cells live on one global lattice anchored at the north-west corner (90 N, 180 W): `row` counts southward, `col` eastward, `cell_id = row * 7200 + col` (3600 x 7200 = 25,920,000 ids, fits int32). Output tables carry `cell_id`, `row`, `col`, `lat_c`, `lon_c` (M-F3-05 only requires the id; the other columns are for joins and human reading). Why this and not another indexing: (a) the id is an identifier only and changes no number in any result; what it governs is joins between phases, reruns, the order used to break ties, and traceability. (b) A country-bounding-box index would renumber a country whenever its polygon or extent changes (the PRT `mainland_only` change of 2026-10-06 would have renumbered every PRT cell) and would not be comparable across countries. (c) A string of center coordinates is stable but heavy and fragile as a join key. (d) A Morton/Z-order code adds spatial locality that nothing here needs. (e) The anchor (90, -180) is an exact multiple of 0.05 (1800 and -3600 steps), so the 0.01 pixels nest 5 x 5 and the 0.1 degree V-07 cells nest 2 x 2 in the same index (`coarse_cell_id`). All arithmetic is on integers (rounded step counts), never on compared float coordinates. A cell on a border has the same id in both countries; the key is (country, `cell_id`), and each country's `cell_area_km2` is its own land area inside the cell. Implementation: `src/geofrea/land_eligibility/cells.py`. The grid it aggregates must be the F2a grid snapped to the 0.05 lattice (`GridNotOnLatticeError` otherwise).
- **D-F3-002 — Engine and parameters (2026-10-07, Douglas's approval of `docs/_audit/2026-10_f3_parameter_research.md`).** Central values in `config/experiments.yaml` `land_availability`: solar slope 10 deg, wind slope 16.7 deg (30%), population density 100 persons/km2, riparian setback 0.5 km, IUCN Ia/Ib/II, `min_eligible_area_km2` 0.1, solar excludes tree cover and cropland (conservative: land in use, Douglas), wind excludes tree cover only; ranges where sourced (wind slope 10.2-30 deg, density 100-200, setback 0.25-1.0 km), the rest held at the central value and declared (OQ-049). Land-cover classes follow ESA WorldCover (10 tree, 40 crop, 50 built-up, 70 snow, 80 water, 90 wetland, 95 mangrove excluded as listed per technology). Phase `land_eligibility` in `main.py` (requires aligned rasters, siting layers, layer registry and the F1b audit report); engine: `land_eligibility/{parameters,fractions,eligibility,pipeline}.py`.
- **D-F3-003 — Sub-pixel shares for E1-E3, independence rule, conservative direction (2026-10-07).** Protected areas, lakes and the riparian setback are measured on 10 x 10 sub-pixels per 0.01 degree pixel (`fractions.py`; riparian by geodesic distance transform, shares kept for the setbacks 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0 km and interpolated in between, never extrapolated), so a 0.5 km setback no longer means "the pixel contains a river" (OQ-045 option c). Shares of different constraints are multiplied as independent; overlaps (a lake inside a setback) are counted twice, so the eligible area is understated. Population density = aligned count / geodesic pixel area (D-F2a-014); a pixel without a population, slope, land-cover or required resource value is not eligible (PRT 0.0%, IND 1.1%, BRA 0.7% of pixels). Shares are cached in `interim/<ISO3>/land_eligibility/` keyed by the source file, grid and thresholds.
- **D-F3-004 — First results with the central parameters (2026-10-07), and what limits them.** Eligible share of the land (E1 partly missing for IND, see below): PRT solar 39.2%, wind 44.2% (3,695 and 3,739 candidate cells of 3,937); IND solar 4.4%, wind 7.4% (23,067 and 29,905 of 105,952); BRA solar 23.1%, wind 29.6% (187,060 and 191,319 of 285,999). Excluded share by constraint (overlapping, any cause), solar / wind: PRT E1 0.8, E2 0.6, E3 24.5, E4 3.4 / 0.1, E5 39.3 / 33.7, E6 15.4; IND E1 0.0, E2 0.6, E3 26.9, E4 5.4 / 2.2, E5 79.8 / 27.5, E6 78.2; BRA E1 7.8, E2 0.6, E3 29.4, E4 0.6 / 0.0, E5 67.2 / 59.1, E6 1.5. Three results are driven by open questions, not by the method: E3 removes a quarter to a third of the land because every stream is a river (OQ-046); E6 removes 78% of IND (OQ-047); E1 depends on the IUCN category set and the IND protected-area file is incomplete (OQ-048). Limits that stay in force: E5 is the land-cover class at one 10 m sample per pixel (a cell rests on 25 samples, about +/-10 points of noise at a 50% share, unbiased); class shares per pixel, computed from the native tiles, are the planned upgrade (approved, after the engine; hours for BRA); slope is a mean per 1.1 km pixel (derived at the DEM resolution, then resampled), lower than the 30-100 m slopes behind the cited thresholds.
- **D-F3-005 — E5 from class counts and the riparian discharge parameter (2026-10-07, M-F2a-06, commit 228f42b; Douglas approved both).** (a) E5 is the share of the pixel's 10 m samples outside an allowed class (F2a counts, D-F2a-015); samples of no class (open sea) count as not allowed; a pixel with no sample at all is invalid. PRT country totals do not move (solar 31.2%, wind 34.9% before and after) while the candidate cells change (3,381 to 3,505 solar, 3,421 to 3,520 wind), as expected: the totals were unbiased, the cells were noisy. (b) New parameter `riparian_min_discharge_m3s` (central 0, range 0-10, `range_set_by_douglas_unsourced`): a stream counts for E3 only if its HydroRIVERS `DIS_AV_CMS` is at least the threshold. Shares are prepared on the grid `riparian_discharge_grid_m3s` = 0, 1, 5, 10 times the setbacks 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 km and interpolated bilinearly; outside the grid is an error. PRT E3 excluded share (share of the land): 12.7 / 24.5 / 45.9% at 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 km for every stream, 9.2% at 0.5 km and 1 m3/s, 3.8% at 5, 2.5% at 10. (c) E4 is now the share of the pixel's 30 m samples above the slope maximum, from Copernicus GLO-30 (D-F2a-016): the DEM F1 holds is a 550 m resample, so the earlier E4 understated the exclusion. PRT: solar 31.2% to 23.9% eligible, wind 34.9% to 31.7%; E4 excludes 31.7% (solar, 10 deg) and 14.0% (wind, 16.7 deg). The literature thresholds' DEM resolution is unknown, so this is the stricter reading and the slope range is where that uncertainty lives (OQ-049).
