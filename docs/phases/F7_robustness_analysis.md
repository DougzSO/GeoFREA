Methodology items: M-F7-01 to M-F7-11, V-03, V-07 (with the `scale` parameter of M-F3-06)

## Contract

Requires: the F6 kernel, inputs, `lcoe_summary_<tech>` (ordering and the `s0` invariant) and `lcoe_nominal_<tech>__<scenario>` of the restrictive and permissive scenarios, `potential_<tech>__<scenario>.parquet` and `candidates_<tech>__<scenario>.parquet` (with `admin1_id`), `forcing.parquet`, `hazard_context.parquet`, `members.yaml`. Produces, per technology and window (`core`, `sensitivity`): `robustness_<tech>__<window>.parquet` and `nominal_lcoe_by_member_<tech>__<window>.parquet`, `futures_`, `draw_statistics_`, `hypothesis_`, `potential_below_tau_` and `hazard_exposure_<tech>__<window>.parquet`, `prim_boxes_<tech>__<window>.parquet` and, for H5, `h5_summary_` and `h5_members_<tech>__<window>.parquet`. An output whose decision parameter or threshold is null is written empty and flagged in its metadata (`geofrea_robustness_skipped`, with the reason and the open question), because the DAG fixes the files.

## Active implementation decisions

Recorded before any code (Douglas's verdicts, 2026-10-08, METHODOLOGY 7.0.0):

- **D-F7-001 — Relative regret and the primary metric (V3; M-F7-02, M-F7-03, V-03).** `r = (LCOE - L*) / L*`. `MR_i` = maximum over climate members of the P90 over parameter samples of `r`. Order: `r` per future `(m, s)`; P90 over samples per member; maximum over members. SR stays secondary. The F7 candidates are the central-set candidates (V1).
- **D-F7-002 — H4 comparison (V4; M-F7-06).** The Jaccard comparison of climate-only versus techno-only futures is primary; the variance decomposition, on relative LCOE, is secondary.
- **D-F7-003 — Feasibility and climate-fragile cells (V9; verdict B of 2026-10-08; M-F7-01, M-F7-02).** A cell with CF below `CF_min` in the nominal future `(m0, s0)` leaves the F7 set. Climate-fragile = feasible in `(m0, s0)` and `CF(m, s0) < CF_min` in at least one member `m`: feasibility that defines the class is evaluated at the nominal parameters `s0` of each member. It is reported separately and not ranked with an infinite MR. Infeasibility that comes only from the parameter samples does not create the class and gives no infinite regret: within the P90 over samples, an infeasible sample receives the regret of the feasible cell with the highest LCOE in that future (finite worst case). `CF_min` is OQ-008.
- **D-F7-004 — One threshold for satisficing and economic potential (verdict A of 2026-10-08, METHODOLOGY 7.0.1).** OQ-052 (cost threshold of economic potential, T-R12, H5) is merged into OQ-008: the LCOE threshold below which potential counts as economic is the satisficing threshold `tau` of M-F7-04, per country and technology.
- **D-F7-005 — Climate-data-invalid cells (D-F5-006; METHODOLOGY 7.1.0, M-F7-01).** The F7 set is the central-scenario candidates present in every member of the core window. A cell with at least one member masked by M-F4-07 forms the class *climate-data-invalid*: it is reported with its count and a map, and it is not ranked and gets no regret. F7 reads `potential_<tech>__central.parquet` only.

Verdicts of 2026-10-09 on `docs/_audit/2026-10_F7_design.md` (METHODOLOGY 7.2.0; D1 to D28 refer to that report, whose section 11 lists them). No production value is entered by these decisions; the synthetic country carries test values only.

- **D-F7-006 — The set of `L*` (D1; M-F7-02).** `L*_f` is the `q_ref` quantile of the LCOE over the feasible cells in `f` of the F7 set `S7`: the central candidates present in `m0` and in every core member and feasible at `f0`. Climate-fragile cells are inside it where feasible; climate-data-invalid cells and cells infeasible at `f0` are counted and mapped, never ranked, and do not set `L*`.
- **D-F7-007 — Feasibility through an energy floor (D2; M-F7-01).** `lcoe_block` takes an optional `min_energy_mwh` per cell: an entry whose sampled energy is below the floor is `+inf` (zero energy is the floor `0`). `CF_min` is applied to the `CF(m, s0)` that F5 stores, not to the draws: F7 passes `+inf` for a cell-member below `CF_min` at `s0` and `0` otherwise, so for a feasible cell-member a draw is infeasible only when its energy is zero (D-F6-007).
- **D-F7-008 — Passes (D3; M-F7-10).** Per member, pass 1 gives the reference level `L*` and the highest finite LCOE per sample over cell blocks; pass 2 gives the regret, its P90 over the draws `s >= 1`, the running maximum over members (`MR`), the satisficing counters, the moments of `X = LCOE / L*` and the potential below `tau` per future. A sample-major pass 3 gives what compares members within one future (H1, H3, the PRIM outcome, the ranges). Nothing sample-level is persisted; pass 3 persists the futures table.
- **D-F7-009 — `q_ref` (D4; OQ-020).** Only `q_ref = 0` is built; any other value raises with OQ-020 named. The sample-blocked quantile for `q_ref > 0` is built when the author asks for it.
- **D-F7-010 — Futures and sub-families (D5; M-F7-03, M-F7-06).** The futures are the core members times the draws `s >= 1`; SR, the P90 and the moments of `X` read the draws only; `f0 = (m0, s0)` stays apart. `MR_clim = max over m of r(m, s0)` and `MR_tech = P90 over s >= 1 of r(m0, s)` rank the cells under the two single-axis families, with the `L*` rule and the ranked set of `MR`.
- **D-F7-011 — Fragile cells (D6; M-F7-01, M-F7-05).** A climate-fragile cell has no `MR`, no `SR`-based rank and no regret in the member where it fails; it stays in the nominal ranking and in the F7 set. The worst-case regret of M-F7-02 applies to the ranked cells in a future where their energy is zero.
- **D-F7-012 — Ranking, ties, `k` (D7; M-F7-05).** Nominal rank: ascending `LCOE_f0`, then ascending `cell_id`. Robust rank: ascending `MR`, descending `SR`, ascending `cell_id`. `k = ceil(p_k / 100 * |S7|)`, at least 1, for both rankings. Ties are exact float equality; no tolerance.
- **D-F7-013 — Truncation and capacity target (D8; M-F7-05, OQ-010).** If the ranked set is smaller than `k`, the robust top-k is the whole set and the outputs carry `top_k_truncated`. The capacity-target top-k accumulates `P_MW` along the robust rank to the target of the technology (`capacity_target_gw`); a target above the capacity of the ranked set takes all of it and sets `target_unreachable`. The target is per technology.
- **D-F7-014 — Hypotheses and rules (D9; M-F7-07, OQ-050).** F7 writes the statistics of H1 to H5 and, for every rule of `experiments.yaml` `hypothesis_rules`, the result of the comparison (`verdict`: `met` or `not_met`). A null rule list gives a warning, the statistic rows stay and carry `verdict = null` with the reason `no pre-registered rule`; the output is marked without verdict. F7 combines no rules into a verdict on a hypothesis and runs no test of significance.
- **D-F7-015 — Ranges over the draws (D10; U-08).** H1, H3 and the PRIM outcome are reported with the central value at `s0` and the P10 to P90 over the draws of the same statistic, from pass 3, with the axis named. Spearman values are computed for every draw at the final sample size.
- **D-F7-016 — Scale (D11; V-07, M-F3-06).** F7 reads the tables of the run scale and holds no scale-specific branch; the 0.1 degree run is the same phases on the 0.1 degree cells (D-F3-013, D-F4-020). H1 to H3 are reported side by side by the caller of both runs. A real 0.1 degree run needs real values.
- **D-F7-017 — 2071-2100 (D12; M-F7-07).** The same code runs on the 18 sensitivity members with its own F7 set, `m0` and `s0` shared and no hazard statistics. The sample size adopted is the core-window one.
- **D-F7-018 — Admin1 (D13; H2).** H2 groups cells by `admin1_id` of the F3 cell table (D-F3-012).
- **D-F7-019 — Hazard exposure (D14; T-R10, H5, OQ-051).** Exposure is computed on the absolute indicator of `hazard_context` for the nine members that carry the hazard channel (three GCMs, 2041-2070); the other members and 2071-2100 are `not available`, never zero (LIMITATIONS L-021). Thresholds are `experiments.yaml` `hazard_thresholds`, null until OQ-051. No 2071-2100 data are acquired (V8).
- **D-F7-020 — Land range (D15; U-08, T-R12, H5).** The potential below `tau` is computed with draws in the central scenario and as a nominal value in the restrictive and permissive ones, from F6's `lcoe_nominal_<tech>__<scenario>` (D-F6-018). The like-for-like series is the F7 set.
- **D-F7-021 — PRIM (D16; M-F7-08).** (The outcome and the descriptors are those of D-F7-054.) Own minimal implementation (peeling and pasting of Friedman and Fisher 1999, settings of Bryant and Lempert 2010 from `experiments.yaml` `prim`); the reference test compares it with `ema_workbench`, generated once in a temporary Python 3.12 environment outside `.venv`, and stored as a fixture with the command that generated it.
- **D-F7-022 — MR and SR (D17; M-F7-09).** Kendall tau-b between `MR` and `-SR` over the ranked set, with the number of tied pairs.
- **D-F7-023 — A null decision parameter (D23; A-09).** F7 refuses to start only without a feasibility rule (`CF_min` for some technology) or with `q_ref != 0`. Otherwise it writes every output that does not need the missing value, lists the skipped outputs with the open question that blocks each, and ends `ran_with_issues`. Dependency: `tau` for SR, T-R12 and H5's cost half; `p_k` for every top-k, H1, H3, H4 and PRIM; the capacity target for the capacity-target top-k and H5; the hazard thresholds for T-R10 and H5's exposure half; the rules for `verdict`. A production run refuses any null (`required_parameters`).
- **D-F7-024 — Maps (D24; A-08).** `lcoe_maps` (after F6) and `robustness_maps` (after F7) follow `settings.yaml` `figures`; rasters are always written. Neither is in `run.target_phases` until the real parameters exist. T-R12 gains the series `fixed_domain` (the cells of `Omega_econ` evaluated in every member): its energy per member is `E_m` of H5, and the dynamic series `like_for_like` stays as the dynamic-domain sensitivity. H5 reads the F5 rows and the F6 nominal LCOE of each land scenario through `thesis_tables.merge_nominal_lcoe` and `like_for_like_cells`, the same cells as T-R12, and the run checks (V-03) that they are the F7 set, that the energies and the nominal LCOE equal those of the F7 inputs and the evaluation, that `E_m` equals the `fixed_domain` rows and that the exposed energy per hazard member equals the T-R10 `any_channel` rows over `economic_domain`. A cell-member below `CF_min` has no finite LCOE in the dynamic domain, as in the evaluation (D-F7-007).
- **D-F7-053 — Cases the text leaves open.** (i) H5b uses the channels that have a threshold and is available when at least one has and a member of the window carries the hazard channel (OQ-051, L-021); a channel without a threshold is excluded and declared (D-F7-058, which replaces the first reading of this item, where one null threshold made H5b not available). (ii) The reference exposure uses the channels that have a reference value of the same quantity (`tx35_days`, `tx40_days`, `rx5day_mm` and the gust); the increment compares the absolute and the reference statistic on those channels only, and `reference_channels` says which. (iii) The maxima over members are over the core members and, for H5b, over the hazard members; `Delta E` and `E_exp` name the member that attains them. (iv) The target has one source, `national_targets` (GW or TWh/year with a horizon); the target of the top-k sensitivity is derived from it (D-F7-059, which replaces the first reading of this item, where `capacity_target_gw` of `parameters.json` was a second setting). (v) With a target of 0 GW `G` is 0 and `CFbar` is null.
- **D-F7-054 — Scenario discovery: unit, outcome and descriptors (OQ-056, resolved 2026-10-14).** The rule is that of `docs/thesis/methods/synthesis_and_scenario_discovery.tex` (METHODOLOGY 13.0.0). The unit is the future `(m, s)` of the core window, `s >= 1`; the sensitivity window has no PRIM (its tables are written empty with `M-F7-08` as the reason). The outcome is `V_f`, the capacity share of the nominal shortlist `S^nom_k` (the `k` cells of the ranked set `I` with the lowest `LCOE_i,f0`, as in H3; `k` of M-F7-05) whose rank within `I` in the future is beyond `k + m`, `m = ceil(prim.tolerance_multiple_of_k * k)` (`k` for the pre-registered 1.0). The rank by relative regret is the rank by `LCOE_i,f` because `L*_f` is common to the cells of a future; an infeasible cell is last and ties go to the lower `cell_id`. `V_f` is computed in the sample-major pass (`futures.py`, `OutcomeShortlist`) and persisted for every future (`futures` column `displaced_capacity_share`), so a change of `theta` needs no rerun of the passes. A case of interest is `V_f >= prim.outcome_threshold = 0.20` (a share within `1e-12` of it is at it). Descriptors: the sampled parameters, `gcm` and `ssp` (categorical) and `delta_rsds_mean`, `dT_mean`, `delta_wind_mean`, now the capacity-weighted means over `S^nom_k` of the member's change factors (they were the means over the F7 set). The settings are `prim` in `experiments.yaml` (`PrimConfig`, "OQ-056, pre-registered 2026-10-14"); the per-country `prim_outcome_share` of `parameters.json` was redundant with `outcome_threshold` and is removed (parameters, registry, schema, decision).
- **D-F7-055 — Scenario discovery: the box and its variants.** `scenario_discovery.py:discover` runs `run_prim` with `peel_alpha` 0.05 and `mass_min` 0.05 and `select_box` chooses, among the boxes of the trajectory with coverage of at least `min_coverage` (0.50) and from one to `max_restricted_descriptors` (3) restricted descriptors, the one of highest density (ties: the larger support, then the earlier box). The whole space is never the answer (it restricts nothing), so `no_eligible_box` can occur when peeling stops at once or the requirement is above what the trajectory reaches. The special cases are `no_cases` (no future of interest: no search), `all_cases` (every future of interest: no search) and `no_eligible_box`. `prim_box_<tech>__core` has one row per setting: `primary`, `theta_0.10` and `theta_0.30` (a new search), `coverage_0.40` and `coverage_0.60` (the primary trajectory with another requirement) and `support_0.02` and `support_0.10` (a new search), each with the status, density, coverage, support, the restricted descriptors and the limits of the box. `prim_boxes_<tech>__core` is the trajectory of the primary search with the box chosen marked `selected`; it is empty in the special cases `no_cases` and `all_cases`.
- **D-F7-056 — Scenario discovery: bootstrap.** `scenario_discovery.py:bootstrap` resamples the futures with replacement `prim.bootstrap_replicates` (200) times and repeats the search with the same settings and rule; the generator is `numpy.random.default_rng` seeded with `derive_seed(sampler.seed)` (a `SeedSequence` of the sampler seed and a fixed tag), and the seed, the number of replicates and the source of the settings are in the provenance of every F7 table (`prim`). With the same seed the replicates are the same. `prim_bootstrap_<tech>__core` is long: the percentiles (`percentile_low`, `percentile_high`, and the median) over the replicates that found a box of the density, coverage and support; for a real descriptor the percentiles of its lower and upper bound over the replicates that restrict it; per descriptor the share of the replicates that restrict it, and per category the share that keep it; and the share of all replicates in each status.
- **D-F7-057 — Cost and tests.** The bootstrap repeats a PRIM search 200 times on the futures of a technology (about the number of members times the draws); the unit and ZZZ tests reduce `bootstrap_replicates` in their copy of the configuration, and the pre-registered value is tested on the shipped file. The staged futures of the unit tests keep the order of the cells, so `V_f` is zero there: the outcome is checked against a brute-force definition in `test_robustness_futures.py`, the search against a synthetic case with a known box in `test_scenario_discovery.py`, and the wiring in `test_prim_pipeline.py` with an outcome that depends on the GCM. L-022 is updated.
- **D-F7-058 — H5b channels: those with a threshold (METHODOLOGY 13.0.1; supersedes D-F7-053 (i)).** H5b uses the hazard channels whose threshold OQ-051 sourced; a channel with a null threshold is excluded and declared, and does not block the others. `build_exposure` keeps the channels with a threshold (`Exposure.channels`) and lists the others in `Exposure.excluded`; `h5_summary` carries both as `channels` (used) and `excluded_channels`, joined by `|`, on every row. H5b is not available only when no channel has a threshold (reason names OQ-051) or no member of the window carries the hazard channel (L-021). The set of channels is fixed when OQ-051 is resolved, before the results; the `channel_<indicator>` sensitivities run over the channels used. T-R10 already skipped a null indicator and keeps doing so, so T-R10 and H5b list the same channels.
- **D-F7-059 — One source for the national target (METHODOLOGY 13.0.1; supersedes D-F7-053 (iv)).** The target is defined once, in `national_targets` of `experiments.yaml`. `capacity_target_gw` is removed from `parameters.json` and from the schemas; `decision.capacity_target_gw` derives the target of the top-k sensitivity (and of the H2 `national_target` variant) from it when its unit is GW, and is null, with the reason in `TechDecision.capacity_target_note`, when the value is null or the unit is TWh/year (no conversion to capacity is registered); the skipped-output list then names OQ-010 and says so. `test_the_target_has_one_definition_the_national_targets_of_experiments_yaml` fails if `parameters.json` or the schemas carry the key again or if src reads it from the parameter block.
- **D-F7-060 — The outcome of PRIM is never replaced in src.** The rule on the GCM that gives the ZZZ run a box (D-F7-057) lives in `tests/synthetic/test_zzz_end_to_end.py` and acts by monkeypatching `pipeline.futures_pass` in the test process. `test_the_outcome_of_a_future_is_never_replaced_in_src_the_gcm_rule_lives_in_tests_only` fails if `pipeline.futures_pass` is not the function of `futures.py`, if a module of `robustness_analysis` or `results_synthesis` other than `futures.py`, `assemble.py` and `table_schemas.py` touches `displaced_capacity_share`, or if these packages name a GCM or contain a monkeypatch.
- **D-F7-061 — F7 uses the members of the technology and repeats the core window without the cells above the sensitivity altitude (2026-10-14, V-B1, OQ-058).** The members of a technology in the F7 set, the futures, the hypotheses and the figures are those that carry the channels of its model, so a GCM whose wind channel is excluded is no member of the wind analysis and its absence does not make a cell `climate_data_invalid`; for solar it is a member and its futures have no wind ratio (`delta_wind_mean` null). For a technology with an altitude sensitivity (wind, 3000 m) the core window is run again with the candidates above it left out, with the whole analysis (classes, rankings, H1 to H5, PRIM) and the tables named `<kind>_<tech>__core_below_3000m`; T-R6 shows its rows. Expected for BRA wind in the core window under the 0.5-1.5 mask: 6 `climate_data_invalid` candidates (GFDL-ESM4), and none for solar.
- **D-F7-062 — The manifest of `cross_country_h4` records the `h4_country` hashes it read (2026-10-14; B-5 of `docs/_audit/2026-10_pre_research_audit.md`).** `outputs/cross_country/manifest.json` (schema 1.1) has `inputs`: `"<ISO3>:h4_country_<tech>__<role>"` -> the hash read, or null where the country registered none. `core/artifact_reader.py::stale_cross_country_inputs` compares them with the country manifests as they are now: a rerun of one country's F7 (another hash), a table registered where there was none, or one lost, makes the verdict stale; a manifest of schema 1.0 (no `inputs`) is stale as a whole.
