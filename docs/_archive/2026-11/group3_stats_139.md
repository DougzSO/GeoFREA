## Method

- **Unit:** the 0.05 degree cell of F3, one row per cell of the table `candidates_*` (the cells with eligible area above zero; 18 tables, none with a missing distance, none without potential).
  The scenarios are the three land-eligibility scenarios of F3: `restrictive`, `central`, `permissive`; the technologies are solar and wind.
- **Distances:** `dist_road_km` and `dist_sub_km` of the table. They are the eligible-area-weighted mean of the uncapped geodesic distance of the pixels of the cell (M-F2b-02, M-F2a-03,
  M-F2a-09), **not the distance of each eligible pixel**: a median over cells is a median of cell means. `dist_sub_km` is the distance to the nearest *eligible* substation, the one F6 bills.
- **Weighting of the medians (items a and b):** the **weighted median** with weight `eligible_area_km2` of the cell, in the scenario and technology of the row: sort the cells by distance
  and take the first distance whose cumulative eligible area reaches half of the total (the lower median at a tie).
- **Share of the eligible area (item a):** the sum of `eligible_area_km2` of the cells with `dist_road_km` > 30 over the sum of all the cells (strictly above).
- **Share of the potential (items b and c):** the potential of a cell is `P_MW`, the installed capacity of `potential_<tech>__<scenario>.parquet` (equal in every member of the climate
  ensemble: checked, the difference between members is zero). A column "energy of m0" repeats the share with `E_MWh` of the member `m0` (the historical climate), which does depend on the
  climate; the shares of other members are not given. A share is the sum of `P_MW` of the cells that meet the condition over the sum of `P_MW` of all the cells.
- **Item b:** cells with `dist_sub_km` > 100 km (strictly above; 100 km is also the `distance_cap_km` of F2b and the long-distance flag of F6, but the distance used here is the uncapped one).
- **Item c:** solar only; the thermal zone of the cell is `pvcz_zone` (Karin et al. 2019, `core/pv_zones.py`, 1 for T1 to 10 for T10), the zone of the nearest PVCZ land point of the cell
  centre within 50 km; T7 or hotter is `pvcz_zone` >= 7. A cell with no zone (value 0) is in the denominator and not in the numerator; its share is in the last column.

## Reading limits

- The numbers are medians and shares of 0.05 degree cells, weighted by area (a, b) or by capacity (b, c); they say nothing about the distribution of the pixels inside a cell, because the
  table keeps only the mean distance of each cell.
- Wind has no thermal-zone column in the table and item c is for solar only, as asked.
- Zone T7 or hotter is a property of the historical climate and does not vary across the members of the climate ensemble (`core/pv_zones.py`).
