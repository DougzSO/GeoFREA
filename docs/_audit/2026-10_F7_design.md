## 0. Summary

1. **F7 is blocked on values, not on design.** Nothing in F7 or F7b can run on a real country until the F5 and F6 values exist (OQ-004, OQ-005, OQ-023; OQ-001, OQ-016 to OQ-019, OQ-022) and F7 has its own decision parameters: `CF_min` and `tau` (OQ-008), `q_ref` (OQ-020, default 0), `p_k` (OQ-021), national targets (OQ-010), hazard thresholds (OQ-051) and the pre-registered decision rules (OQ-050). All of these are `null` today. Everything below can be built and tested on the synthetic country with test values.
2. **Two-pass evaluator, as D1 of F6 fixed.** Per member, pass 1 gives the reference level `L*_f` per future over the F7 set (a running minimum when `q_ref = 0`); pass 2 gives `r`, the P90 over draws, the running maximum over members, the satisficing counters and the moments of the relative LCOE. Every statistic of M-F7-03, M-F7-04, M-F7-06 (both parts) and the nominal ranking comes from these two passes and the `s0` column; no sample-level array is stored. Hypothesis ranges over the draws (H1, H3, PRIM outcome) need a third, sample-major pass (D3, D10). The kernel needs one small additive extension, an energy floor per cell, so that feasibility by `CF_min` is evaluated inside the kernel (D2).
3. **Measured cost, BRA wind (172,250 F7 cells, 19 members: 18 core and `m0`)**: about **2.5 minutes at 500 draws and about 20 minutes at 4,000**, both passes and all accumulators, one technology and one country; the sensitivity window adds about the same again. Peak memory is **about 3.1 blocks of the cell-by-sample array** (measured), so with `memory.max_batch_gb = 1` a block holds 62,000 cells at 500 draws and 7,800 at 4,000; the state kept across members is under 100 MB (section 2.6). **The F6 report's time estimates were too low** (section 2.7): measured, F6 for BRA wind takes about 5 minutes at 500 draws and about 42 minutes at 4,000, not 3 and 22.
4. **Gaps found in earlier phases (section 1.3), each with a decision:** F3 does not persist the per-pixel exclusion shares E1 to E6, which M-F7b-01 needs (D18); the cell table has no `admin1` column, which H2 needs (D13); `hazard_context.parquet` exists only for the 9 members of the three ISIMIP3b models in the core window, which bounds T-R10 and H5 (D14); F6 reads only the central land scenario, so the land range beside T-R12 needs a nominal-only LCOE for the other two (D15); the 0.1 degree table of F3 holds areas only, so V-07 needs a derived 0.1 degree candidate table and a rerun of F4 to F7 (D11).
5. **PRIM (D16): recommend a minimal own implementation** with a reference test against `ema_workbench`'s output stored once. `ema_workbench` 3.0.0 needs Python 3.12 or newer and five packages the project environment (Python 3.11) does not have; the standalone `prim` 1.0.0 is GPL-3.0-or-later and the repository has no license file. T-O1 is optional, so this is the cheapest reversible choice.
6. **F7b (D18 to D22):** the four metrics are fresh; V-06 is guaranteed by four tests (module allow-list, DAG sink, a perturbation test that reruns F5 to F7 with and without the plant file and compares them byte for byte, config scan). The ZZZ country gets a synthetic inventory flagged `synthetic`, refused in a production run.
7. **28 decisions** for the author's verdict in section 9, each with options and a recommendation, including tie-breaking, a top-k larger than the ranked set, and what F7 writes when `tau`, `CF_min` or `p_k` is null (D23).

### 1.3 Findings of the inventory (to fix at implementation or by a METHODOLOGY line)

1. **Feasibility by `CF_min` is not what F6 computes.** The kernel returns `+inf` only for zero energy (D-F6-007). M-F7-01 needs `CF(m, s) >= CF_min`. The condition is equivalent to an energy floor, `E_c(s) >= P_c * 8760 * CF_min`, so one optional argument of the kernel closes it (D2).
2. **F3 keeps no per-pixel exclusion shares.** M-F7b-01 asks for the share of existing capacity "in excluded pixels, per exclusion constraint". F3 persists the combined eligible fraction per pixel and, per 0.05 degree cell, the excluded areas E1 to E6. The pixel-level E1 to E6 exist inside F3's run and are discarded (D18).
3. **No admin1 assignment.** H2 compares dispersion with the range across admin1 units. GADM admin1 is an active layer (M-F1-02) but no table carries an admin1 identifier (D13).
4. **Hazard coverage is partial by design.** `hazard_context.parquet` has rows for 9 members (three GCMs with an ISIMIP3b counterpart, three SSPs, window 2041-2070); the 2071-2100 window has none (S-05, D-F4-004). T-R10 and H5's exposure half therefore cover three of the six GCMs and the core window (D14).
5. **Land range of H5 and T-R12.** F6 and F7 read only the central scenario (V1); U-08 asks for the land range beside the climate effect. A nominal-only LCOE for the restrictive and permissive scenarios (no draws) is enough and cheap (D15).
6. **M-F7-05 leaves three things open:** the ties (the text says "ties broken by SR" for MR only), the base of the top-k count (candidates, F7 set, or ranked set), and what happens when the ranked set is smaller than the top-k. Decisions D7 and D8.
7. **M-F7-06's two top-k sets are not defined.** "Top-k under climate-only futures `(m, s0)`" needs a criterion over the 18 members of a single sample, and "top-k under techno-only futures `(m0, s)`" one over 500 samples of a single member. D5 defines them as the max-regret of those two sub-families, which keeps M-F7-03's form.
8. **U-04 and the provisional MR.** The F6 protocol uses a stand-in for MR with feasibility by positive energy. F7's definitive MR uses `CF_min`, so the stand-in is replaced as soon as F7 exists and the adopted sample size must be recomputed with it (D27); the draws of the protocol are independent between sizes.
9. **The D3 rule (draws only) already covers the P90.** METHODOLOGY 7.1.3 states it; SR and the moments of `X` follow the same set: draws `s >= 1` of the 18 core members; `f0 = (m0, s0)` stays apart (D5).
10. **F6 time estimates were too low** (section 2.7); the F6 record should carry the measured figures.
11. **The plant-file guard of V-06 is a regular expression over `src/`** (`test_v06_no_other_module_imports_the_tracker`); F7b must be added to its allow-list in the same change that creates F7b, with the stronger tests of D21 beside it.

## 2. The evaluator

### 2.1 Sets and futures

- **Candidates** `C`: the central-scenario candidates (V1). **F7 set** `S7`: the cells of `C` present in `m0` and in every member of the core window (no masked cell-member, M-F7-01, D-F5-006) **and** feasible at `f0 = (m0, s0)`. A cell absent from the core members is *climate-data-invalid* and a cell infeasible at `f0` is *infeasible at f0*; both are counted and mapped, neither is ranked, neither enters `L*`. The sensitivity window has its own set, defined the same way over its 18 members (D12).
- **Futures** `f = (m, s)`: the 18 core members times the draws `s = 1..N`. `f0 = (m0, s0)` is the nominal present future. The climate-only family is `(m, s0)` for the 18 members; the techno-only family is `(m0, s)` for the `N` draws. Sample 0 is column 0 of every block; the statistics that use draws (`P90`, `SR`, moments of `X`) read columns `1..N`; columns are extracted before any in-place partition.
- **Climate-fragile**: a cell of `S7` with `CF(m, s0) < CF_min` in at least one core member (M-F7-01). Its failing members are stored as a bitmask (at most 37 members fit an `int64`) and decoded to a list when the table is written.

### 2.2 Pass 1 (per member): the reference level

For each member, over blocks of cells with all `N + 1` samples: compute the LCOE block with the energy floor (infeasible entries are `+inf`), then update, per sample, the running minimum `L*` (`q_ref = 0`) and the running maximum of the finite entries `H`. The result is two vectors of length `N + 1` per member; the block is released. With `q_ref > 0` the quantile over cells must see all cells of one sample, so the pass is sample-blocked (D4).

### 2.3 Pass 2 (per member): regret and accumulators

For each block again: LCOE block, `r = (L - L*)/L*` in place, infeasible entries replaced by the finite worst case `(H - L*)/L*` of their sample (M-F7-02; fragile cells keep their infeasible entries out of the P90 rule: D6), then, from the same block:

| Accumulator (per cell unless stated) | Update | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| nominal LCOE at `(m, s0)` | column 0, stored per member (cells x members) | nominal rank (`m0`), H1, climate-only, T-R12 |
| `MR_clim` | running max over members of `r(m, s0)` | M-F7-06 primary |
| `P90_m` of `r` over draws | partition of columns `1..N`, exact | `MR` (running max over members) |
| `MR_tech` | `P90` of `r(m0, s)` over draws, from the `m0` pass | M-F7-06 primary |
| SR counter | count of draws with feasible and LCOE `<= tau`; divided by `18 N` | M-F7-04, M-F7-05 ties, M-F7-09 |
| moments of `X = LCOE / L*` | per member: sum and sum of squares over draws; across members: running sums of `E_s[X]`, `E_s[X]^2` and `Var_s[X]` | M-F7-06 secondary (law of total variance, exact) |
| fragile bitmask and count | `CF(m, s0) < CF_min` | M-F7-01 |
| potential below `tau` per (member, sample) | `(LCOE <= tau and feasible).T @ P_MW` and `... @ E_MWh`, vectors of length `N + 1` | H5, T-R12 |

Everything above is a reduction of the block; the largest persistent state is the cells-by-members nominal LCOE (172,250 x 19 x 8 B = 26 MB) and a dozen vectors of the cell count.

The exact identity `Var(X) = E_m[Var_s(X)] + Var_m[E_s(X)]` holds for equally weighted members and samples; the accumulators above compute both terms without storing the cells-by-members moments.

### 2.4 Pass 3 (sample-major, for ranges and PRIM)

H1, H3 and the PRIM outcome compare rankings *within one future* across members, and U-08 asks for the range of a statistic over the draws. That needs, for each draw `s`, the LCOE of all members at once (`18 x C` values, 25 MB for BRA wind), their top-k sets (a partition, 1.6 ms for 172,250 cells) and, for Spearman, their ranks (an argsort, 21 ms). The pass recomputes the kernel (the same cost as pass 2) and emits per future a few numbers (Jaccard with the nominal top-k, share of nominal top-k cells leaving, Spearman between SSP pairs of the same GCM), so the persisted result is the futures table of D25: `M x N` rows (9,000 at 500 draws), not a cell-level array (D3, D10).

### 2.7 Correction to the F6 report

Section 4.4 of `2026-10_F6_design.md` estimated 95 Melem/s for the kernel and 35 and 93 Melem/s for the quantiles and the moments. The committed implementation measures 42 Melem/s for the kernel (it evaluates the annuity factors with `expm1` and `log1p` once per sample, and the energy block with an outer product) and 14.5 Melem/s for the whole F6 summary. For BRA wind (6.7 M cell-members) F6 therefore takes about 5.2 minutes at 500 draws (kernel 80 s, summary 231 s) and about 42 minutes at 4,000, not 3 and 22. No code is affected; the F6 record should quote the measured figures. Optimizing `summarize_draws` (it makes five passes over the block) is possible later and is not needed for any decision here.

## 3. Rankings, top-k and outputs

### 3.1 Rankings (M-F7-05)

- **Nominal rank**: ascending LCOE at `f0` over `S7` (fragile cells included: they are feasible at `f0`). **Robust rank**: ascending `MR`, then descending `SR`, over the ranked set `R7 = S7` minus the climate-fragile cells.
- **Top-k size**: `k = ceil(p_k * |S7|)`, at least 1, the same for both rankings so the Jaccard of H3 compares sets of one size (D7). The nominal top-k is cut from `S7`, the robust top-k from `R7`; a fragile cell in the nominal top-k counts as leaving it.
- **Ties**: exact float equality only, then the next key, finally ascending `cell_id` (A-12). No tolerance is introduced (D7).
- **Truncation**: if `|R7| < k`, the robust top-k is all of `R7`, flagged `top_k_truncated` in the table metadata and the hypothesis rows; the statistics are still reported (D8).
- **Capacity-target top-k (OQ-010)**: along the robust rank, cumulate `P_MW` until the national target of the technology is met; the cells so taken are the strategy-level top-k, reported with their count and share. A target above the total potential of `R7` takes all of `R7` and sets `target_unreachable` (D8). The target is per technology; a joint solar-plus-wind target would need a joint ranking, which M-F7 does not define.

### 3.2 Per-future sub-families

`MR_clim_i = max over m of r_i,(m,s0)` and `MR_tech_i = P90 over s of r_i,(m0,s)` rank the cells under the two single-axis families; both are non-negative and use the same `L*` rule as `MR` (D5). The primary H4 quantity is `Jaccard(top-k nominal, top-k by MR_clim)` against `Jaccard(top-k nominal, top-k by MR_tech)`, with `k` as above.

| `prim_boxes_<tech>.parquet` | one per box | limits per descriptor, coverage, density, support |
| masks and classes | rasters | section 8 |

Provenance JSON in every parquet: parameters, `CF_min`, `tau`, `q_ref`, `p_k`, seed, N, window, scale, kernel version, `provisional: false`.

## 4. Hypothesis tests (M-F7-07)

F7 computes statistics; the rule (statistic and threshold, OQ-050) is read from `experiments.yaml` (a new `hypothesis_rules` block, all `null` until the author fills it) and F7 writes `verdict` only when the rule is present (D9). All statistics use the F7 set, the central land scenario and `s0` for the central value; the range over draws and its axis come from pass 3 (D10).

### 4.1 Per hypothesis

| H | Statistic | Inputs | Where OQ-050 enters |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Spearman of the nominal LCOE ranking of `S7` between SSP pairs (per GCM: 6 GCMs x 3 pairs; and between the per-SSP medians over GCMs); Jaccard of the top-k sets between SSPs; share of the nominal top-k cells that leave the top-k under at least one core member; repeated for 2071-2100 | `nominal_lcoe_by_member`, `p_k`, members' `gcm` and `ssp` | threshold on the share and on the Jaccard that counts as "substantial" and "non-trivial" |
| H2 | IQR over median of `MR` across cells; range of the admin1 medians of `MR`; both against the `P_MW`-weighted national `MR` | `MR`, `P_MW`, `admin1_id` (D13) | the dispersion ratio at which the aggregate "misrepresents" |
| H3 | Jaccard(top-k nominal, top-k robust); share of robust top-k capacity outside the nominal top-k; distance between the `P_MW`-weighted centroids of the two sets (geodesic, `core/geodesy.py`) | `MR`, nominal rank, `P_MW`, `lat_c`, `lon_c` | the Jaccard and share thresholds |
| H4 | per country and technology, `J_clim = Jaccard(top-k nominal, top-k by MR_clim)` against `J_tech`; the ordering of the countries by `J_clim - J_tech`; secondary: variance shares | sub-families (3.2), moments of `X` | the ordering rule ("no country-independent ordering": which statistic over three countries) |
| H5 | per member, national potential (GW, TWh) with feasible LCOE `<= tau`, climate effect against `m0` on the like-for-like series; share of potential in cells above each hazard threshold; both against the national target | pass 2 vectors, `potential_aggregates`, `hazard_context`, targets (OQ-010), thresholds (OQ-051) | the margin that "matters" relative to the gap between potential and target |

A test of significance is not proposed: the cells are spatially dependent and the draws are not a sample of a population; the statistics are descriptive, as the pre-registered rules of OQ-050 are thresholds on them (D9).

### 4.2 Repetition for 2071-2100

The sensitivity window uses the same code with the 18 sensitivity members and its own F7 set (more cells are climate-data-invalid there: for BRA wind 18,541 cells have a masked member in any of the 37 members against 9,027 in the core window). `m0` and `s0` are shared. The hazard-based statistics are not repeated (no hazard rows, S-05). Rows carry `window`.

### 4.3 Repetition at 0.1 degree (V-07)

H1 to H3 are recomputed at 0.1 degree and reported beside the 0.05 degree values (MAUP). What must be recomputed:

| Phase | Needed for 0.1 degree | Reuse |
|---|---|---|
| F1, F1b, F2a, F2b | nothing | all pixel layers |
| F3 | a 0.1 degree candidate table: `eligible_area_km2` and `excluded_area_km2_Ek` are sums of the 0.05 degree cells (exact, `CoarseCellRow`); the resource attributes and distances are eligible-area-weighted means and so are the exact eligible-area-weighted means of the 0.05 degree means; candidacy (`min_eligible_area_km2`) is applied to the 0.1 degree sum; the 0.1 degree centre coordinates; today `cells_0p1deg_<tech>` has the areas only | derive from `cells_<tech>__<scenario>`; no pixel rereading |
| F4 | change factors interpolated to the 0.1 degree centres (bilinear, M-F4-04) and the wind mask M-F4-07 recomputed; the GCM native fields are reused | the monthly climatologies |
| F5 | the same functions on the 0.1 degree table; capacity is additive, the capacity factor is computed from the aggregated means (that difference is what V-07 measures) | code unchanged |
| F6 | the same kernel and design (same seed and N) | code unchanged |
| F7 | the same passes (4 times fewer cells: BRA wind about 43,000) | code unchanged |

The mechanism is a `scale` option of the run (`0p05` or `0p1`) that selects the candidate table and an artifact suffix; no phase function changes (D11). The cost is that of a quarter-size run per phase.

## 5. T-R10 (extended) and T-R12

### 5.1 T-R12: potential below `tau` against the targets

Per member and technology: `GW_below_tau` and `TWh_below_tau` over the like-for-like series (the F7 set, the same cells in every member, D-F5-006), at `s0` as the central value and the range over the draws from pass 2 (U-08), compared with the national target (OQ-010) as a gap; the land range is the nominal value over the three land scenarios (D15). The same quantities over the all-present series are reported beside them. `tau` is `satisficing_lcoe_usd_per_mwh` (OQ-008, merged OQ-052). A cell is counted when it is feasible and its LCOE is at most `tau`.

### 5.2 T-R10: exposure of potential to hazards

Per hazard indicator and member with the hazard channel: the potential (GW, TWh) in cells above the exposure threshold (OQ-051), for all of `S7`, for the nominal top-k and for the robust top-k, by SSP, GCM-averaged where three GCMs allow, window 2041-2070 only. The wording is "exposed" (M-F4-05). The members without the hazard channel have no value and are shown as not available, never as zero (D14). The indicators are absolute values with their `*_ref` columns in the same row; the threshold is applied to the absolute value, so exposure of the reference climate is also reported, which makes the climate change in exposure readable. Thresholds, units and tiers live in `experiments.yaml` `hazard_thresholds` (all `null`, OQ-051).

## 6. PRIM (M-F7-08) and MR against SR (M-F7-09)

### 6.1 The outcome

PRIM needs one outcome per row. M-F7-08 names the outcome "nominal top-k cell leaves top-k", which is per cell. The proposal is one row per future `f = (m, s)` with the outcome "the share of the nominal top-k cells that are outside the top-k of `f` is at least `theta`" (binary), `theta` being the author's value (a new open question; for example the median of the observed shares is a data-derived alternative that makes the box describe the worse half of the futures). The share, not the binary, is persisted in `futures_<tech>`, so the threshold can change without a rerun. Descriptors: the sampled parameter values, the country-mean change factors of the member (`delta_rsds`, `dT`, `delta_wind`), SSP and GCM (categorical). With 500 draws and 18 members that is 9,000 rows; at 4,000 draws 72,000.

### 6.3 MR against SR (M-F7-09)

Kendall's tau-b between the `MR` ranking and the `SR` ranking over `R7` (`scipy.stats.kendalltau`, tau-b, since SR has many ties at 1.0), per country and technology, reported with the number of tied pairs; T-O2 plots the two rankings. Cost: seconds. SR ranks higher is better and MR lower is better, so the statistic is computed between `-SR` and `MR` and the sign is stated (D17).

## 7. F7b external_validation

### 7.1 Inputs and common rules

Plants: `status == operating`, inside the mainland polygon (already clipped, OQ-039), with their `capacity_mw`, `start_year` and `location_accuracy`. A plant is assigned to its 0.01 degree pixel (M-F7b-01) and to its 0.05 degree cell (M-F7b-02). Nothing computed here is read by any other phase except F8 (V-06).

### 7.2 The four metrics

| Item | Definition proposed | Inputs |
|---|---|---|
| **M-F7b-01** | for each constraint Ek, the capacity-weighted mean excluded share of the pixel under the plants (primary, continuous), and the capacity share in pixels with an excluded share above 0, at least 0.5 and equal to 1 (sensitivity); the same for the combined eligible fraction; per technology and country; plants outside the country grid are reported by count | per-pixel Ek rasters (gap, D18), GEM |
| **M-F7b-02** | enrichment ratio: capacity share in the lowest nominal-LCOE decile (and the lowest two, three) divided by the eligible-area share in the same decile; deciles are eligible-area-weighted quantiles of the nominal LCOE at `m0` over the candidates, so the denominator is 10 percent by construction; plants in non-candidate cells are reported separately and excluded from the numerator and the denominator, with their share of capacity | F6 `lcoe_nominal` at `m0`, F3 eligible area, GEM |
| **M-F7b-03** | a table: for each country and technology, the F5 central-scenario technical potential at `m0` (GW, TWh, with the land interval over the three scenarios), next to published national estimates, each with source, year, definition (technical, economic, resource), and a column that says whether the definition matches F5's; no number is computed from the published values | published estimates (gap, D20), F5 aggregates |
| **M-F7b-04** | the reading rule is a note in the table metadata and in the thesis table: plausibility evidence, not accuracy | text |

### 7.3 Vintage and accuracy

Plants differ in vintage (auction, policy, grid access) and in location accuracy. The proposal reports M-F7b-01 and M-F7b-02 for all operating plants and, as sensitivity, for plants with `start_year` from a configured year onwards and with an `approximate` location excluded; the year and the accuracy classes are configuration with the reason recorded, and no value is chosen here (D19).

### 7.4 V-06 by test

1. **Allow-list:** `ALLOWED_IMPORTERS` of `test_gem_trackers.py` gains the F7b module and no other.
2. **Sink:** a test over the registered `PhaseSpec`s: no phase except `results_synthesis` requires an F7b artifact.
3. **Perturbation:** a synthetic-country test runs F5, F6 and F7 twice, once with the plant file present and once with it removed, and requires byte-identical tables. This is the test that fails if a plant-derived value reaches any computation.
4. **Config scan:** the existing regular expression over `config/*.yaml` stays; the published-potential file of D20 is named so that it is not caught by it and not read by F3 to F7.

### 7.5 The synthetic inventory

`scripts/generate_zzz_fixture.py` writes `raw/gem/ZZZ/gem_solar_wind_ZZZ.parquet` and its `gem_snapshot.json` with `synthetic: true`: a handful of plants per technology placed by construction — some in the cheapest cells, some in the exclusion bands (the ZZZ land cover has cropland, grassland and tree-cover bands), some outside the candidate set, one with an approximate location — with capacities that make each metric a hand computation. A production run refuses an inventory flagged synthetic, as it refuses a synthetic power curve (D22).

## 9. Decisions for the author's verdict

**D1. The set over which `L*` is taken.** Options: (a) `S7`, fragile cells included when feasible in the future; (b) `R7` (without the fragile cells); (c) all candidates. Recommendation: **(a)**. M-F7-02 defines `L*_f` over "feasible cells in `f`"; a fragile cell is feasible in many futures, excluding it would change the reference without a reason, and (c) would let climate-data-invalid cells, which have no regret, set the reference.

**D2. Feasibility inside the kernel.** Options: (a) `min_energy_mwh` per cell, an optional argument of `lcoe_block` (zero energy is the case `0`); (b) F7 computes its own CF block and masks; (c) a second kernel. Recommendation: **(a)**, additive and tested in F6's V-02 style; `min_energy_mwh = P_MW * 8760 * CF_min`. (b) doubles the outer products; (c) forks the kernel.

**D3. Passes.** Options: (a) two member-major passes plus a sample-major pass 3 for the quantities that compare members within a future (H1, H3, PRIM, ranges); (b) two passes only and statistics at `s0`; (c) persist per-future top-k sets. Recommendation: **(a)**. (b) cannot give the range over draws that U-08 asks for; (c) stores `C x M x N` bits (172,250 x 18 x 500 = 194 MB per technology) and is forbidden in spirit by M-F6-04. Pass 3 persists only the futures table.

**D4. `q_ref > 0`.** Options: (a) build only `q_ref = 0` now and refuse any other value with the OQ named; (b) build the sample-blocked sort now. Recommendation: **(a)**: OQ-020 proposes to test {0, 0.01} and to adopt 0 unless outliers dominate; measure first. The cost of (b) is +60 percent on passes 1 and 2 and a different block axis in pass 1 (section 2.2).

**D5. Futures, sample set and sub-families.** Recommendation: futures are the 18 core members times draws `s >= 1`; SR, the P90 and the moments of `X` read draws only; `f0` is separate; `MR_clim = max_m r(m, s0)` and `MR_tech = P90_s r(m0, s)` are the criteria of M-F7-06's primary comparison, computed with the same `L*` rule. Alternatives: include `s0` in SR (inconsistent with D3 of F6) or define the climate-only ranking by the median over members (changes M-F7-03's form). Needs a METHODOLOGY line in M-F7-06 (PATCH).

**D6. Fragile cells in the regret.** Options: (a) `MR` is null for a fragile cell, its infeasible futures are not given the worst-case regret, and it stays in the nominal ranking; (b) give it the worst-case regret like any infeasible cell. Recommendation: **(a)**, which is what M-F7-01 and M-F7-05 say (not ranked with an infinite `MR`; robust rank over the cells that are not fragile). The worst-case rule of M-F7-02 then applies to non-fragile cells that are infeasible only at some draws, which is the case it was written for.

**D7. Ranking, ties, top-k size.** Recommendation: nominal rank ascending LCOE; robust rank ascending `MR`, then descending `SR`, then ascending `cell_id`; `k = ceil(p_k * |S7|)` for both rankings; exact float equality for ties, no tolerance. Alternative: `k` from `|R7|` for the robust set only (the two sets then differ in size and the Jaccard needs a convention). A METHODOLOGY line in M-F7-05.

**D8. A top-k larger than the ranked set; capacity-target top-k.** Options for `|R7| < k`: (a) take all of `R7` and flag `top_k_truncated`; (b) raise. Recommendation: **(a)**: a high fragile share is a result, not a failure (it is reported in the class counts); raising would stop a country for a finding. For the OQ-010 sensitivity: cumulate `P_MW` along the robust rank to the per-technology target; a target above the potential takes all of `R7` and sets `target_unreachable`. Options for the target's scope: per technology (recommended), or joint, which needs a joint ranking M-F7 does not define.

**D9. Hypothesis statistics and the decision rule.** Options: (a) F7 writes statistics and, when the rule of OQ-050 is in `experiments.yaml`, a `verdict`; F8 reads both; (b) F7 writes statistics only and F8 applies the rule; (c) F7 refuses to run without the rules. Recommendation: **(a)**: the rule is pre-registered configuration, the evaluation is a comparison, and a null rule leaves `verdict` null without blocking. No test of significance (section 4.1).

**D10. Range of a statistic over draws (U-08).** Options: (a) central at `s0` and the P10 to P90 over draws of the same statistic, computed in pass 3, with the axis named; (b) central only. Recommendation: **(a)** for H1 and H3 and the PRIM outcome; the Spearman values are computed for every draw at the final sample size only (the cost is about 32 minutes for BRA wind at 4,000 draws), and the Jaccard values at every size. A `techno-economic` range for H1 means: how much the statistic moves when the parameters are drawn.

**D11. The 0.1 degree scale check.** Options: (a) a `scale` option of the run that selects a derived 0.1 degree candidate table and an artifact suffix, with F4 interpolation redone at the new centres and the same code in F5 to F7; (b) separate 0.1 degree phase copies. Recommendation: **(a)** (section 4.3). F3 gains a derivation function (exact sums and eligible-area-weighted means from the 0.05 degree cells table) and keeps its pixel data untouched. The MAUP effect enters through the capacity factor computed from aggregated means, which is intended.

**D12. The 2071-2100 repetition.** Recommendation: the same code with the 18 sensitivity members and its own F7 set; `m0` and `s0` shared; no hazard statistics. The adopted sample size (U-04) is the core-window one.

**D13. Admin1.** Options: (a) a support table `cell_admin1_<ISO3>.parquet` written by F7 (cell centre in the GADM admin1 polygon, geometry intersection, never a bounding box; a centre in no polygon takes the polygon of largest overlap with the cell and is counted); (b) an `admin1_id` column in F3's cell table; (c) assignment by the largest overlap only. Recommendation: **(a)** now, because it needs no change of F3 and the data stays valid for 0.05 and 0.1 degree; (b) is cleaner if F3 is touched anyway for D18, and costs a METHODOLOGY line in M-F3-03.

**D14. Hazard exposure.** Recommendation: thresholds in `experiments.yaml` `hazard_thresholds` per indicator with unit, source and tier, all `null` until OQ-051; exposure computed on absolute values for the members with the hazard channel; members without it reported as not available; no exposure statement for 2071-2100; a GCM-average only over the hazard GCMs, with the number of GCMs stated. Alternative: acquire the 2071-2100 hazard data (183 GB, M-F4-05): not recommended before OQ-007 says a hazard enters LCOE.

**D15. Land range and the like-for-like series for T-R12 and H5.** Options: (a) F6 writes `lcoe_nominal_<tech>__<scenario>.parquet` for the restrictive and permissive scenarios (nominal parameters, every member, no draws); (b) F7 calls the kernel on those scenarios itself; (c) report the land range only for the potential and not for the potential below `tau`. Recommendation: **(a)**: it keeps F8 from recomputing (M-F8-01) and costs two nominal passes (negligible). The like-for-like series is the F7 set, as in D-F5-006.

**D16. PRIM outcome and implementation.** Outcome: one binary per future, "share of nominal top-k leaving at least `theta`", with `theta` the author's new value, and the share persisted so `theta` can change (alternative: Jaccard with the nominal top-k below a threshold, equivalent). Implementation: option **(c)**, own minimal, with a stored reference fixture (section 6.2). Peeling `alpha` and the minimum support (`mass_min`) are configuration with the source recorded (Bryant and Lempert 2010), no value here.

**D17. MR against SR.** Kendall tau-b between `MR` and `-SR` over `R7`, tie counts reported, sign stated.

**D18. F7b exclusion layers.** Options: (a) F3 persists, for the central scenario, the per-pixel excluded share of each constraint E1 to E6 as COGs (`excluded_share_E<k>_<tech>.tif`, about 70 MB each for BRA, twelve per country); (b) F7b recomputes them from the F2b layers (a second implementation of M-F2b-01, rejected by M-F8-01's spirit and by the risk of divergence); (c) use the cell-level `excluded_area_km2_Ek / cell_area_km2` at the plant's cell (coarser than "pixels"). Recommendation: **(a)**, as an additive F3 output (METHODOLOGY line in M-F3-05 and a rerun of F3 for the three countries, no change of any existing result). (c) is a fallback and is stated as such in the table.

**D19. Enrichment and vintage.** Recommendation: eligible-area-weighted deciles (denominator 10 percent), plants in non-candidate cells reported apart, all operating plants for the main table and the vintage and accuracy filters of section 7.3 as sensitivity rows whose year and classes are configuration with a stated reason.

**D20. Published estimates.** Options: (a) `config/published_potential.yaml` with source, year, definition and tier per entry, collected by the author, not read by F3 to F7; (b) a table in the F7b record; (c) a CSV outside the repository. Recommendation: **(a)**, with a schema check and a test that only F7b reads it. The values are the author's (a new open question: sources per country and technology).

**D21. V-06 guarantees.** The four tests of section 7.4, in particular the perturbation test.

**D22. Synthetic inventory.** As section 7.5; flagged `synthetic`, refused in a production run.

**D23. A null decision parameter.** Options: (a) F7 refuses to start when `CF_min`, `tau` or `p_k` is null (all-or-nothing, A-09); (b) F7 refuses only without `CF_min` and `q_ref` (the core of the evaluator) and writes every table that does not need the missing value, listing the skipped outputs and the OQ that blocks each, with the manifest status `ran_with_issues`; (c) F7 fills nothing and writes what it can silently. Recommendation: **(b)**, with (a) in a production run (`--production` already refuses a null uncertain parameter; it gains the F7 values). The dependency map: `CF_min` is needed by everything; `tau` by SR, T-R12 and H5's cost half; `p_k` by every top-k and H1, H3, H4; the targets by the capacity-target top-k and H5; the hazard thresholds by T-R10 and H5's exposure half; the rules by `verdict`. (c) is rejected.

**D24. Maps.** As section 8; both support phases not in `run.target_phases` until the real parameters exist.

**D25. Outputs.** As section 3.3, including the futures table and `nominal_lcoe_by_member`; the per-cell column names are final only after the verdicts.

**D26. Module boundaries.** `robustness_analysis` imports `lcoe_modeling.kernel`, `design`, `sampling`, `summary` and a new neutral `lcoe_modeling/inputs.py` (the readers moved out of `pipeline.py`), never `lcoe_modeling.pipeline` or `convergence`. F7 has no technology name (A-04). F7b imports nothing from F5 to F7 except the table schemas.

**D27. The memory budget of `memory.max_batch_gb` governs F7 as it governs F6.

**D28. Phases and DAG.** `robustness_analysis` requires `potential_<tech>__central`, `forcing`, `forcing_masked`, `members`, `hazard_context`, `land_eligibility` and the F6 tables only for the invariant `s0` column equals `lcoe_nominal`; produces the files of 3.3. `external_validation` requires `land_eligibility`, `lcoe_summary_<tech>` and the GEM raw file; produces the validation tables. Support phases `lcoe_maps`, `robustness_maps`. None in `run.target_phases` until the real parameters exist.

### 10.2 Test plan (summary)
